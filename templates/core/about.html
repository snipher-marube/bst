Our Story

Born from a Simple Observation

In 2026, our founder noticed a recurring problem while consulting for small and medium enterprises: businesses were drowning in spreadsheets but starving for insights. Excel files were passed around, numbers were manually entered, and critical decisions were based on outdated information.

We realized that while enterprise companies had teams of data scientists and million-dollar BI tools, SMEs were left with either expensive custom development or complex tools that required technical expertise.

That's why we built {{ site_settings.site_name }} — a no-code analytics platform that lets anyone create custom tracking systems, generate insights, and make data-driven decisions without writing a single line of code.

"We believe that data insights shouldn't be a luxury reserved for large corporations. Every business, from the local farm to the growing e-commerce store, deserves to understand their numbers and make better decisions."
